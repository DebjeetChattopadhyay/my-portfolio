Our Story

Born in Kolkata,
Loved in Bangalore

B2B Biriyani started with a dream — to bring the legendary Kolkata-style biriyani to the streets of Bangalore. Unlike any other biriyani, the Kolkata version is defined by its perfectly cooked whole potato, the gentle warmth of aromatic spices, and meat so tender it falls off the bone.

Our founder, a Kolkata native, couldn't find the real thing in Bangalore — so he brought the recipes, the spices, and the heart of Kolkata's iconic dhaba culture to create B2B Biriyani. Every pot we cook is a love letter to parar biryani.
